A study app that teaches you by asking questions, then turns the same topic into a quiz, flashcards or a voice session. I started from [Vercel's AI chatbot template](https://vercel.com/templates/next.js/nextjs-ai-chatbot) and added the tutor prompt, quizzes, flashcards, uploads, sign-in and voice on top. It's live, you just need to sign in.

## What it does

First it asks what you want to learn, your level and what you already know. Then it explains things with examples, gives you a hint when you're stuck and ends each reply with a question. You can also attach a JPEG, PNG or PDF (up to 5MB).

Right from the chat, it can make a quiz or flashcards on the same topic. Voice sessions run on [LiveKit](https://livekit.io/agents), and the transcript lands in the same chat.

## How it works

Quiz answers stay on the server until you submit, so no peeking. All the model calls go through the [Hack Club AI](https://ai.hackclub.com/) proxy.
