AI Tutor is a study app. It teaches by asking questions, then turns the same topic into a quiz, flashcards or a voice session. The repo started from [Vercel's AI chatbot template](https://vercel.com/templates/next.js/nextjs-ai-chatbot), which gave the documents, version history and suggestions. I added the tutor prompt, quizzes, flashcards, uploads, sign-in and voice.

The app sits behind sign-in and the repo is private.

## How it works

The tutor asks what you want to learn, your level and what you already know. It explains with examples, gives a hint when you're stuck and ends its replies with a question. You can attach a JPEG, PNG or PDF up to 5MB, and math renders with KaTeX.

From the chat, the model can make a quiz or flashcards for the same topic. Questions are streamed to the browser with the answers and explanations stripped out. A server route loads the saved quiz, checks it's yours and grades each answer. Every model call goes through the [Hack Club AI](https://ai.hackclub.com/) proxy.
