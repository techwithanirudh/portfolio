A study app that teaches you by asking questions, then turns the same topic into a quiz, flashcards or a voice session. It's live (you just need to sign in), though honestly it's on life support right now.

## How it started

Back in 2023, when the first OpenAI models came out, I was so amazed by the ChatGPT UI that I built my own clone on Firebase, [AnirudhGPT](/projects/anirudh_gpt). This was back when I built everything on Replit (RIP, I'll miss you Replit). A lot of students ended up using it to ask study questions, and that gave me an idea: what if I turned it into a tutor? I'd been reading about RAG, so the dream was that you could upload a textbook and get help with it.

The first version was built on top of AnirudhGPT and ran on Firebase. Then it got a few rewrites and moved onto [Vercel's AI chatbot template](https://vercel.com/templates/next.js/nextjs-ai-chatbot), where I added the tutor prompt, quizzes, flashcards, uploads, sign-in and voice. Along the way I sent a few changes back to the AI chatbot itself and met some amazing people like [N1cklas](https://github.com/N1cklas) and [Nishimiya](https://github.com/Nishimiya) in the (unofficial) Vercel Discord server. It even got a mobile app at one point, though that got scrapped, sadly. It was a good run!

## What it does

First it asks what you want to learn, your level and what you already know. Then it explains things with examples, gives you a hint when you're stuck and ends each reply with a question. You can also attach a JPEG, PNG or PDF (up to 5MB).

## How it works

Quiz answers stay on the server until you submit, so no peeking. All the model calls go through the [Hack Club AI](https://ai.hackclub.com/) proxy.
