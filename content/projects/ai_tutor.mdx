AI Tutor is a study app. It teaches by asking questions, then turns the same topic into a quiz, flashcards or a voice session. It's live at tutor.techwithanirudh.com behind a sign-in, and the repo is private. It started from [Vercel's AI chatbot template](https://vercel.com/templates/next.js/nextjs-ai-chatbot). I added the tutor prompt, quizzes, flashcards, uploads, sign-in and voice.

## What it does

The tutor asks what you want to learn, your level and what you already know. It explains with examples, gives a hint when you're stuck and ends its replies with a question. You can attach a JPEG, PNG or PDF up to 5MB.

From the chat, it can make a quiz or flashcards for the same topic. Voice sessions run on [LiveKit](https://livekit.io/agents), and the transcript is saved to the same chat.

## How it works

Quiz answers stay on the server until you submit, so you can't peek at them. Every model call goes through the [Hack Club AI](https://ai.hackclub.com/) proxy.
