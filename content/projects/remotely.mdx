Remotely turns a TV remote into the mouse for a Mac connected to a TV. It's a menu bar app for macOS 15 and later, and it's live. You can download it from the GitHub releases page. It's ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so the first launch needs right-click and Open.

## What it does

Arrows move the pointer, and holding speeds it up. Centre clicks, twice double-clicks and a hold right-clicks. Back twice switches the arrows to scrolling. You need an HDMI cable, HDMI-CEC switched on in the TV settings and the Accessibility permission, and the first run walks you through all three.

## How it works

A TV with HDMI-CEC already passes its remote's presses to whatever is on its HDMI input. Remotely reads those presses and turns them into pointer movement, clicks, scrolling and shortcuts. Updates arrive through [Sparkle](https://sparkle-project.org/).
