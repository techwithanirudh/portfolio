Remotely turns a TV remote into the mouse for a Mac connected to a TV. A TV with HDMI-CEC already passes its remote's presses to whatever is on its HDMI input, and Remotely turns them into pointer movement, clicks, scrolling and shortcuts. It's a menu bar app for macOS 15 and later. It's ad-hoc signed, not notarized, so the first launch needs right-click and Open.

## How it works

Arrows move the pointer, and holding speeds it up. Centre clicks, twice double-clicks and a hold right-clicks. Back twice switches the arrows to scrolling. Setup is an HDMI cable, HDMI-CEC switched on in the TV settings and the Accessibility permission, and the first run walks you through all three.

Apple's CoreRC never delivered a single press, even though the daemon received 204 button events in one test. So Remotely runs `log stream` against `corercd` and reads the raw CEC button codes out of each line. Those log lines aren't a public interface. A later rewrite back to CoreRC broke remote detection completely.

Back twice scrolls, so a single Back has to wait for a second press before it acts. I cut that wait from 380ms to 300ms.
