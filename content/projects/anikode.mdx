AniKode is a drag-and-drop website builder on [GrapesJS](https://grapesjs.com/). You drag blocks onto a page, add custom HTML and JavaScript when you need more control, and keep several pages in one project. I built it three times, and the latest is AniKode Studio. Its landing page and sign-in are live, and the editor needs an account. The 2022 landing page is still online too.

## What it does

You create a project, add pages and open one in the editor. Blocks sit on the left, and styles, settings, layers and pages are on the right. Every change is saved as you go, and the editor tells you if a save fails. Sign-in is an emailed link or GitHub, through [NextAuth](https://next-auth.js.org/) and [Resend](https://resend.com/), and pages are saved to Postgres with [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/).
