Daily Diary was a journal app I built in the summer of 2022, with [React](https://react.dev/) and [Bootstrap](https://getbootstrap.com/). Every entry was its own dated page, styled like a page in a book, and instead of staring at a blank page you got little prompts, like what you're thankful for or what you learned today.

## What it was like

It also had a social side. The helper on the login page sums it up: you can write your daily diary in the app and share it with your friends.

Sign-in went through [FirebaseUI](https://github.com/firebase/firebaseui-web) on top of [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) Authentication, which hands you a ready-made sign-in flow, and I switched on Google, Facebook, GitHub, email and phone. Five ways to log in to a diary is maybe a lot, but FirebaseUI made each one pretty much a checkbox.

## The helper

The fun bit was the helper. A button on the login page opened a five-step intro to the app, one speech bubble at a time. Next to it was a mic button, and pressing it made the browser read the current step out loud with the Web Speech API's speech synthesis, no audio files or extra services needed.

## What happened to it

It used to live at diary.techwithanirudh.com, but it's offline now. The Internet Archive only kept the login page, so sadly that's the only screen left to show here.
