Transcript Seeker records a meeting with a bot or takes an uploaded file, then gives you a transcript you can search and ask questions about. Lazare Rossillon at Meeting Baas started it in July 2024 and built the viewer, search and chat. I did most of the work from October 2 to November 5, 2024.

It's open source under the MIT license and lives in the Meeting BaaS org. The last change was in May 2025, and the hosted app wasn't loading when I checked on October 24, 2026.

## What it does

[Meeting Baas](https://www.meetingbaas.com/) sends bots into calls, and [Gladia](https://www.gladia.io/) or [AssemblyAI](https://www.assemblyai.com/) transcribe uploads. You bring your own keys, and OpenAI answers your questions. Click a word in the transcript and it jumps to that moment.

## How it works

Keys, meetings, notes and chats are saved in a database inside your browser, so the basic app needs no server. I swapped Lazare's IndexedDB store and Express server for [PGLite](https://pglite.dev/) with [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/) on top.
