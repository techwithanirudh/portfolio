Transcript Seeker records a meeting with a bot or takes an uploaded file, then gives you a transcript you can search and ask questions about. Lazare Rossillon at Meeting Baas started it in July 2024 and built the viewer, search and chat. I did most of the work from October 2 to November 5, 2024.

It's MIT licensed and lives in the Meeting BaaS org.

## How it works

You bring your own keys. [Meeting Baas](https://www.meetingbaas.com/) sends the bots into calls, [Gladia](https://www.gladia.io/) or [AssemblyAI](https://www.assemblyai.com/) transcribe uploads, and OpenAI answers your questions. Click a word in the transcript and it jumps to that moment.

Keys, meetings, notes and chats live in a Postgres database inside the browser, so the basic app needs no server. I swapped Lazare's IndexedDB store and Express server for [PGLite](https://pglite.dev/) with [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/) on top.

A [Turborepo](https://turborepo.dev/) holds the React web app, a [Nitro](https://nitro.build/) proxy for the Meeting Baas API, and a [Hono](https://hono.dev/) API with [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com/) for Google sign-in and the upcoming meetings page. The web app and proxy run on [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/), the API on Cloud Run.
