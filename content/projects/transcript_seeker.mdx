Transcript Seeker is a project I did for Meeting BaaS, and I think it was my first one there. The idea was simple: one app where you can manage and look through all your Meeting BaaS meetings, read the transcripts and ask questions about them.

## What it does

[Meeting Baas](https://www.meetingbaas.com/) sends bots into calls, and [Gladia](https://www.gladia.io/) or [AssemblyAI](https://www.assemblyai.com/) transcribe uploads. You bring your own API keys, and OpenAI answers your questions. Click a word in the transcript and it jumps to that moment.

## How it works

The fun part is the architecture. It's a fully client-side SPA with no backend of its own. Everything lives in [PGLite](https://pglite.dev/), which is Postgres running right in your browser, with [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/) on top. Your keys, meetings, notes and chats all stay on your machine. The only extra piece is a tiny [Nitro](https://nitro.build/) proxy that passes requests through to the Meeting BaaS API. The UI is built with [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/).
