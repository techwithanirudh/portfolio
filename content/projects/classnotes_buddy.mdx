Classnotes Buddy lets you upload class notes and share them with a link, so if you miss a class you can just grab the notes from a classmate. I've built it twice. The 2022 version started as Web Dev Simplified's Google Drive clone tutorial, which I rewrote and built on, and in 2024 I rebuilt it as Notes Buddy.

Both are still live: the 2022 version at notes-buddy.vercel.app and the rebuild at notes.techwithanirudh.com.

## What it does

You sign in with email or Google, upload files and sort them into folders. Anyone signed in can browse, but only the uploader can rename or delete. In the 2022 version, new uploads also wait for an admin to approve them.

## How it works

The 2022 version runs on [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) with [Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/) for search, and the rebuild runs on [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) with [tRPC](https://trpc.io/). In the rebuild, uploads pick up where they left off if your connection drops.
