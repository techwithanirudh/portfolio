Depensa is an expense tracker that reads your order history from the shops you buy from and puts it all in one list. There's no bank account or card to link. It's live, and it runs on Android and iPhone. It's not on the [App Store](https://www.apple.com/app-store/), so on iPhone you use [SideStore](https://sidestore.io/) or [AltStore](https://altstore.io/).

## What it does

It supports 100 shops across ten regions, and you can add another by its web address. Each order becomes an expense in your currency. It also spots repeating charges, lets you set monthly limits, and will email you a CSV if you ask.

## How it works

You sign in on the shop's own page inside the app, so Depensa never sees your password. The first time through a shop, an AI model writes a small reader for its orders, and every sync after that replays it on a schedule. If a site has no usable orders, the app says it can't read it.
