Depensa is an expense tracker that reads your order history from the shops you buy from and puts it all in one list, with no bank account or card to link. On Android you can get it from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.techwithanirudh.depensa). It's not on the [App Store](https://www.apple.com/app-store/), so on iPhone you install it with [SideStore](https://sidestore.io/) or [AltStore](https://altstore.io/).

## How it started

The first plan looked pretty different from what shipped, but one idea survived every rewrite: AI only *learns* how to read a shop, it doesn't do the daily syncing. A model browsing on every sync costs money every time and answers a bit differently each run, so the everyday part had to be plain code.

## Building it

Sign-in happens on your phone. The shop's own login page opens inside the app, you log in yourself, and only the session goes to the backend, where it's stored encrypted. Depensa never sees your password.

The first time you add a shop, an agent figures out where your orders live and saves what it learned. After that, syncing just replays it, with no model in the loop. Every order is converted to your currency when it's saved, so it all lands in one total.

## Where it is now

It works with 100 shops across ten regions. On top of the list you can move orders between categories, set a monthly limit for a category or a shop, and ask for an email with all your expenses as a CSV. Version 1.1.0 added home screen widgets for this month, your budget and what's coming up.

Next on my list: an Expo SDK upgrade, moving the widgets onto `expo-widgets` and adding Live Activities, and having the big shops in every country ready ahead of time, so the first person to add one doesn't have to wait.
