Depensa reads your order history from the shops you buy from and puts it in one list. There's no bank account or card to link. It runs on Android and iPhone.

It has 100 shops across ten regions, and you can add another by its web address. It's not on the [App Store](https://www.apple.com/app-store/), so on iPhone you use [SideStore](https://sidestore.io/) or [AltStore](https://altstore.io/).

## How it works

You pick a shop and sign in on the shop's own page inside the app, MFA included. Depensa never sees your password. The backend keeps the session you get back, encrypted, for later syncs. The worker pulls its jobs from [Convex](https://www.convex.dev/), so it needs no public address.

The first time through a shop, an AI model pokes around the signed-in site once and writes a reader. That's a small JSON file saying where the orders are, which fields to grab and how to page. Every sync after that just replays the reader on a schedule, and a model only comes back to fix a reader that broke. If a site has no usable orders, the app says it can't read it.

Each order becomes an expense in your currency. It also spots repeating charges, lets you set monthly limits, and will email you a CSV if you ask.
