AI agents that hop into a Google Meet or Teams call and actually talk. I built the first version in November 2024, and the Meeting BaaS team kept going from there, adding personas and a hosted API. It's open source under MIT and lives in their org now.

## What it does

You give it a meeting link and pick a persona, and the agent joins the meeting and holds a spoken conversation. Personas are just Markdown files with the agent's traits, voice and first line.

## How it works

[Meeting BaaS](https://www.meetingbaas.com/) joins the call and streams its audio over a WebSocket. In my version, a small proxy turned those audio frames into something [Pipecat](https://www.pipecat.ai/) could read, and Pipecat ran the rest: [Deepgram](https://deepgram.com/) transcribed what people said, an OpenAI model wrote the reply and [Cartesia](https://cartesia.ai/) spoke it back. A script started each bot and proxy pair with its own [ngrok](https://ngrok.com/) tunnel, so a few bots could join at once.
