AI agents that join a Google Meet or Teams call and talk in it, each with its own personality. I wrote the first version in November 2024, and the Meeting BaaS team added more after that. It's in the Meeting BaaS org now, MIT licensed.

## How it works

[Meeting BaaS](https://www.meetingbaas.com/) joins the call and streams its audio over a WebSocket. [Pipecat](https://www.pipecat.ai/) runs the voice pipeline. Silero spots speech, [Deepgram](https://deepgram.com/) transcribes it, an OpenAI model writes the reply and [Cartesia](https://cartesia.ai/) speaks it back.

The two sides don't speak the same language. Meeting BaaS sends plain audio bytes and Pipecat wants protobuf frames, so a small proxy wraps each chunk going in and strips the frames coming out. Everything runs at 16 kHz.

My version ran each bot as three processes, and `batch.py` started as many as you asked for, each with its own [ngrok](https://ngrok.com/) tunnel. Today a Markdown file sets each agent's personality, and a FastAPI server starts the bots. Meeting BaaS hosts it as an API.
