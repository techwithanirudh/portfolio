Maskio is a little pixel-art platformer I made during the pandemic. You play a masked hero who jumps across platforms, stomps viruses and hunts for a vaccine, and the start screen proudly calls it the Corona Virus Superhero Game. It's open source under AGPL-3.0, and you can still play it in the browser.

## How it started

Back then I built pretty much everything on Replit, and Replit had a template for [Kaboom](https://kaboomjs.com/), a small JavaScript game library. Maskio started from that template. If you dig through the history, the project was originally called `mario-updates` and loaded its assets from a `mario` subdomain, so yeah, you can probably guess what I was riffing on.

The first commits on GitHub are from the end of November 2021, and they're mostly me fighting with sprite sizes: make the images smaller, scale them down again, then make the player bigger. The vaccine came in a couple of days later, which is when the game finally had a way to fight back.

## What it does

You get 20 seconds to reach the portal in each world. Hit blocks from below for a coin, an apple that makes you twice as big (and jump higher) for six seconds, or the vaccine. Land on a virus to squash it, but walk into one and it's game over. Once you've got the vaccine, space fires syringes at them.

## How it works

Each world is just a text grid where every character is a tile: `=` is a block, `^` is a virus, `+` is the portal, and `%` and `*` are the blocks with a coin or a power-up inside. Kaboom turns the grid into a level. The viruses use a tiny custom patrol component that walks them sideways and flips their direction whenever they bump into something, and the apple is another small component that scales the hero up, swaps in a bigger jump and counts the six seconds down.

## Moving it off Replit

In April 2024 I came back and made it run without Replit. That meant dropping the Replit config and the Replit Database package, deleting a multiplayer WebSocket server that only ever said "oh hi", downgrading Kaboom and fixing whatever broke, and finally renaming the package from `mario-updates` to `maskio` (the commit is literally called "maskio branding lol"). I also redid the phone controls. The hero runs right on his own, and one tap jumps, two shoot and three stop him so you can steer by hand.

For most of its life Maskio had just two worlds. In August 2025 I added ten more, so now there are 12.
