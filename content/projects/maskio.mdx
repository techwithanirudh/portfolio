Maskio is a small pixel-art platformer from the pandemic years. A masked hero jumps across platforms, dodges viruses and picks up a vaccine. It started from Replit's [Kaboom](https://kaboomjs.com/) template and is open source under the AGPL-3.0.

The game is live, and the site still loads and plays in the browser.

## What it does

You get 20 seconds to reach the portal in each of 12 worlds. Hit blocks from below for coins, an apple that makes you twice as big, or the vaccine. Landing on a virus kills it, and walking into one ends the game. With the vaccine, space fires syringes.

## How it works

Each world is a text grid where every character is a tile. On a phone the hero runs right by itself, one tap jumps, two shoot and three stop the run.
