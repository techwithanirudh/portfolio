Maskio is a small pixel-art platformer from the pandemic years. A masked hero jumps across platforms, dodges viruses and picks up a vaccine. It started from Replit's [Kaboom](https://kaboomjs.com/) template and still plays in the browser. It's open source under the AGPL-3.0.

## How it works

Each world is a text grid where every character is a tile. `=` is grass, `^` a virus, `+` the portal to the next world. Blocks you hit from below drop a coin, an apple that doubles your size for six seconds, or the vaccine. You get 20 seconds per world.

Landing on a virus mid-jump kills it, but one that walks into you ends the game. With the vaccine, space fires syringes.

On a phone the hero runs right by itself. One tap jumps, two shoot and three stop the run.
