A portfolio template with work sections, a blog and shadcn/ui. You swap in your own details and write the content in MDX. The live demo runs as John Doe, a made-up developer. Yeom-JinHo sent two fixes in PRs #56 and #57, a dialog scroll-lock cleanup and a compact-header menu fix.

It's a GitHub template under the MIT license.

## How it works

You start a copy with `create-next-app` pointed at the repository. Blog posts and work pages are MDX files that [Fumadocs](https://fumadocs.dev/) renders. Each homepage section reads its text from its own config file, and most come in cozy, modern and compact layouts that one index file picks.

The contact form uses [Resend](https://resend.com/) and checks a [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/) token on the server. Without keys it says it's misconfigured instead of failing on submit. Sign-in is [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com/) with Google and GitHub, and blog comments sit in Postgres through [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/).
