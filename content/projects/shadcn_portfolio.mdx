A good-looking portfolio template built on shadcn/ui. To make it easy to make your own, every section of the site comes as a few blocks (cozy, modern, compact and a couple more), so you pick a layout per section and swap in your details. It's a GitHub template under MIT, and the live demo runs as John Doe, a made-up developer.

## How it started

The first commit went in at the end of March 2024, and the second one was "Migrate to typescript (debatable)", which is a very honest commit message. Early on it was all vibes: an animated blob in the background, a custom cursor, a contact form that sent through [Resend](https://resend.com/) (and taught me that email clients don't render SVGs). The README credits a long list of sites that inspired it, from leerob.io to ped.ro.

## From one layout to blocks

A few days in, I started a second "horizontal" layout next to the original vertical one. In June 2024 I renamed them to cozy and compact, and the template slowly turned into a set of blocks instead of one fixed site. Modern came that August (starting with testimonials), and minimal joined later. Today the hero, about, experience, projects, testimonials, contact and more each have their own folder of variants.

Some ideas came and went. A preloader that counted from 1 to 100 got cut as "kind of like a distraction", then came back as an option for the cozy layout. Page transitions went in in September 2024 and came out again in March 2025.

## The blog and comments

The blog showed up in September 2024 and moved to [Fumadocs](https://fumadocs.dev/) MDX that November, with a table of contents soon after. In January 2025 I moved everything to Tailwind v4. Then in March 2025 I added [Fuma Comment](https://github.com/fuma-nama/fuma-comment): readers sign in with Google or GitHub through [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com/) and leave comments right under a post, saved to Postgres with [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/).

## How it works

Comments brought auth, a database and emails along, and they landed together with the move to a [Turborepo](https://turbo.build/repo) (yeah, I kinda over-engineered this one): the site lives in one app, and auth, comments, the database, emails, validators and the UI kit are their own packages. The pre-Turborepo version still lives on the `tailwind-v4` branch if you want something simpler.

You start a copy with `create-next-app` pointed at the repo, and each section reads its text from its own config file. The contact form sends through Resend and checks a [Cloudflare Turnstile](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/) token on the server.
