A portfolio template with work sections, a blog and shadcn/ui. You swap in your own details and write the content in MDX. It's a GitHub template under the MIT license, and the live demo is up. The demo runs as John Doe, a made-up developer.

Yeom-JinHo sent two fixes in PRs #56 and #57, a dialog scroll-lock cleanup and a compact-header menu fix.

## What it does

The homepage has sections for projects, testimonials and a contact form, and most come in cozy, modern and compact layouts. Visitors can sign in with Google or GitHub to comment on blog posts.

## How it works

You start a copy with `create-next-app` pointed at the repository. Blog posts and work pages are MDX files that [Fumadocs](https://fumadocs.dev/) renders, and each section reads its text from its own config file. The contact form sends through [Resend](https://resend.com/). The single-app version stays on the `tailwind-v4` branch.
