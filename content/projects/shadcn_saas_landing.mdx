A SaaS marketing site in one project: landing page, pricing, blog, newsletter and a contact form. It's a GitHub template under the MIT license, and the live demo is up. The demo is branded as a made-up product called SaasCN, and the README names [next-forge](https://next-forge.com/) and RunFunRun as inspiration.

## What it does

Pricing has monthly and yearly tabs and a plan comparison table. The blog has tags, RSS, search and comments. The contact and newsletter forms send email through [Resend](https://resend.com/).

## How it works

Blog posts are MDX files that [Fumadocs](https://fumadocs.dev/) indexes for search. Pricing plans live in one data file that the plan cards and the comparison table both read.
