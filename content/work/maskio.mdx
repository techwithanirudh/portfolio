Maskio is a pixel-art platformer. Jump across platforms, avoid spikes, and collect coins across multiple worlds.

## Why it exists

I built Maskio to learn KaboomJS by making a complete game with levels, sprites, scenes, and collisions.

## How it works

KaboomJS scenes define the levels and collisions for platforms, hazards, and coins. The game supports keyboard controls on desktop and touch controls on mobile. esbuild builds it, and Express serves it in development and production.

## Stack

- KaboomJS, Node.js, Express.
- esbuild, nodemon.
- Lightweight scripts for build and serve.
