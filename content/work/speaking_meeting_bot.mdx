What if a meeting bot could actually sound like someone, not just transcribe what was said? Speaking Meeting Bot does that: a Python FastAPI service that spins up real-time voice agents with distinct personas, then sends them into Google Meet or Teams calls through the MeetingBaaS APIs.

Each persona lives in a Markdown file, so its behavior, tone, and knowledge can be tweaked without touching any code. Audio runs through Pipecat, combining speech-to-text and text-to-speech providers with OpenAI for the reasoning, and it can run behind a local ngrok tunnel or a production URL depending on where it's deployed.
