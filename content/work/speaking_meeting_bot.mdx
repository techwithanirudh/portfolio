Speaking Meeting Bot deploys AI agents that can join Google Meet or Teams calls with distinct personalities.

## Why it exists

A meeting bot that just transcribes is only half useful. This one actually participates, with a personality you can shape, so it fits a specific role in a call instead of being a generic assistant bolted on.

## How it works

Each agent's behavior, tone, and knowledge are defined in Markdown files you can edit without touching code, which makes it easy to tune a persona without redeploying. A FastAPI service exposes REST and WebSocket endpoints for spawning bots, and MeetingBaaS APIs handle actually joining the call. The audio pipeline runs through Pipecat, combining speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and OpenAI for reasoning, with ngrok for local development and a standard base URL setup in production.

## Stack

- FastAPI, Pipecat, Python 3.11.
- MeetingBaaS APIs + WebSocket transport.
- Cartesia TTS, Gladia/Deepgram STT, OpenAI.
