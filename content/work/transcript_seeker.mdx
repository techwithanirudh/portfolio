Transcript Seeker connects to Zoom, Google Meet, and other platforms through the MeetingBaaS API, or you can just upload your own audio or video files.

## Why it exists

Meeting recordings are easy to make and hard to actually use afterward. This gives them a proper interface: synced playback and transcript, search, and an AI you can ask about what was said, instead of scrubbing through a video hoping to find the right moment.

## How it works

The interface syncs playback with the transcript, so clicking any word jumps you to that moment in the recording. MeetingBaaS bots handle joining and recording meetings automatically, while AI chat and summarization tools let you search or explore a transcript conversationally. It's local-first, using PGLite in the browser with optional Turso/SQLite for persistent calendar data, all within a Turborepo workspace with a Nitro proxy and Drizzle ORM behind the scenes.

## Stack

- React + Vite, Tailwind, Radix UI.
- Nitro proxy, Drizzle ORM, PGLite/Turso.
- Turborepo + pnpm workspace tooling.
