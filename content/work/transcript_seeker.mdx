Transcript Seeker records meetings from Zoom, Google Meet, and other platforms, or lets you upload an audio or video file. You can read the transcript and ask questions about it.

## How it works

The transcript follows the recording, so clicking a word jumps to that moment. MeetingBaaS bots can join and record meetings. AI chat and summaries help you search the transcript. PGLite stores data in the browser, with optional Turso/SQLite storage for calendar data.

## Stack

- React + Vite, Tailwind, Radix UI.
- Nitro proxy, Drizzle ORM, PGLite/Turso.
- Turborepo + pnpm workspace tooling.
