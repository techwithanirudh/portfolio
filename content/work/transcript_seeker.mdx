Scrubbing through a meeting recording to find the one thing someone said is tedious. Transcript Seeker fixes that by syncing playback with the transcript: click a line, jump to that moment in the audio. Uploading, transcribing, and browsing all happen in a React and Vite interface, backed by a Nitro proxy and Drizzle models.

Storage is local-first, using PGLite right in the browser, with Turso or SQLite available for calendar data that needs to stick around. MeetingBaaS APIs bring meeting bots into the mix, and built-in AI chat and summarization tools let you ask questions about a transcript instead of just reading through it.
