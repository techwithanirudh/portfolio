AI Tutor is a learning app that turns studying into a chat. Ask it a question and it can walk you through the answer, quiz you on it, or turn it into flashcards, so the same material gets reinforced a few different ways instead of being read once and forgotten.

Voice sessions let you go through a study session out loud instead of typing, and progress is saved so you can pick a topic back up later. Sign-in is handled through NextAuth, and the underlying AI can switch between a few different model providers.
