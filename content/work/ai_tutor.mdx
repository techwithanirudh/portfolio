AI Tutor is a learning app that turns studying into a chat. Ask it a question and it can walk you through the answer, quiz you on it, or turn it into flashcards, so the same material gets reinforced a few different ways instead of being read once and forgotten.

## Why it exists

Most study tools make you pick one format upfront: flashcards, or quizzes, or notes. But the way you want to review a topic changes depending on how well you already know it. This ties chat, documents, quizzes, and flashcards together so you can move between them without starting over.

## How it works

A chat workspace sits at the center, with versioned documents you can edit and get suggestions on. From any topic you can generate a quiz or a set of flashcards without leaving the conversation. Voice sessions let you go through a study session out loud instead of typing, with a transcript-aware UI keeping track of where you are.

Sign-in is handled through NextAuth, history is saved so you can pick a topic back up later, and the underlying AI can switch between a few different model providers.

## Stack

- Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind, Radix UI.
- Drizzle ORM + Neon, NextAuth, React Email.
- AI SDK providers with a typed model registry.
