AI Tutor is a study app. Ask a question, get an explanation, and turn the same topic into a quiz or a set of flashcards.

## Purpose

Chat, notes, quizzes, and flashcards live in one place. You can change how you study without starting the topic again somewhere else.

## How it works

You can edit documents, ask questions in chat, and get suggestions while you study. You can also make quizzes and flashcards from a topic, or use a voice session instead of typing.

NextAuth handles sign-in, and study history is saved so you can come back later. The app can use different AI model providers.

## Stack

- Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind, Radix UI.
- Drizzle ORM + Neon, NextAuth, React Email.
- AI SDK providers with a typed model registry.
