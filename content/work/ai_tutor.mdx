Studying alone with just a chat window gets old fast. AI Tutor turns the chat into an actual study loop: a conversation can become a quiz, a set of flashcards, or a document you keep editing. Behind the scenes, a shared prompt registry decides whether OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google handles each request.

Auth and chat are kept separate, and everything is saved through Drizzle on Neon, so a session picks up right where you left off. Sign-in runs through NextAuth with Google, Resend, and Keycloak. There's also a voice mode: LiveKit handles the audio, keeps the transcript in sync, and filters out background noise, so studying out loud works the same way as typing.
