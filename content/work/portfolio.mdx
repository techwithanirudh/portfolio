This site, the one you're reading right now, treats MDX as the source of truth for every work and blog entry. Fumadocs handles the collections, search, and schema validation behind the scenes, and on top of that sits OG image generation, RSS feeds, and structured metadata, so posts and projects are easy to share and easy to find.

Cards, dialogs, and layout sections are built with Tailwind and Radix. Drizzle and Better Auth back the comments and newsletter features. Content, metadata, and UI are kept in separate modules, so each piece can change without dragging the others along with it.
