This is my personal site, where I write, share my work, and collect guestbook notes.

## How it works

Blog posts and project pages are MDX files that Fumadocs loads and indexes for search. Drizzle and Better Auth handle the guestbook and comments. The AI chat uses the site's content to answer questions about my work. The site also generates social images, an RSS feed, and a contact form backed by React Email and Resend.

## Stack

- Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Radix UI.
- Fumadocs MDX and Shiki, Zod validators.
- Drizzle ORM, Better Auth, React Email and Resend.
