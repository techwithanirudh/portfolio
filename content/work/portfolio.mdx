This is my personal site. I use it to write, share my work, and let people leave a note in the guestbook.

## Why it exists

I wanted my site to be more than a resume. It has a blog I can update by adding an MDX file, a guestbook, project pages, an AI chat about the site, and a few smaller pages.

## How it works

Blog posts and project pages are MDX files, loaded and searched through Fumadocs. Drizzle and Better Auth handle the guestbook and comments. The AI chat uses the site's content to answer questions about my work. The site also has generated social images, an RSS feed, and a contact form.

## Stack

- Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Radix UI.
- Fumadocs MDX + Shiki, Zod validators.
- Drizzle ORM, Better Auth, React Email + Resend.
