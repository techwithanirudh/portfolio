This is the site you're looking at right now. It's a Next.js app that reads its content straight from Markdown files, blog posts and project write-ups alike, with search built on top so both are easy to find.

## Why it exists

A portfolio is only as good as how easy it is to keep updated. Writing a new post or project should mean adding a Markdown file, not touching a database or a CMS dashboard, so content stays as easy to edit as the rest of the codebase.

## How it works

Fumadocs turns the `content/` folder into typed collections with search indexing built in. On top of that sit the boring-but-useful parts: preview images generated automatically for social shares, an RSS feed for the blog, and a contact form (plus comments and a newsletter signup) backed by Drizzle and Better Auth.
