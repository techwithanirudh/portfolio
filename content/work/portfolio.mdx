This is my personal site: where I write, show what I've built, and let people leave a note in the guestbook. You're looking at it right now.

## Why it exists

Most personal sites are a static resume that goes stale after a month. I wanted mine to actually be worth coming back to: a blog I'd keep writing in because publishing is just adding a Markdown file, a guestbook so visitors leave something behind instead of just reading and leaving, and enough small extras (an AI chat that can answer questions about my work, a uses page, a colophon) that it feels like a real place rather than a template with my name swapped in.

## How it works

Blog posts and project write-ups both live as Markdown, turned into typed, searchable collections by Fumadocs. The guestbook and comments run through Drizzle and Better Auth, so visitors can sign in and leave a message or reply to a post. There's an AI chat that's aware of the site's own content, so you can ask it about a project instead of digging through pages yourself. Around that sit the smaller things: OG images generated automatically for social shares, an RSS feed for the blog, and a contact form backed by React Email and Resend.

## Stack

- Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Radix UI.
- Fumadocs MDX + Shiki, Zod validators.
- Drizzle ORM, Better Auth, React Email + Resend.
