A SaaS product usually needs both a marketing site and a docs hub, and stitching those two together is annoying. This template does it for you: a Next.js 16 marketing site paired with Fumadocs-powered docs, styled with shadcn/ui and Tailwind, with a typed content pipeline and a few motion touches underneath.

For teams that need a data layer, Drizzle scripts and Better Auth hooks are already in place, along with React Email and Resend for onboarding or contact flows. The goal is a solid starting point that covers both the landing page and the docs without much extra setup.
