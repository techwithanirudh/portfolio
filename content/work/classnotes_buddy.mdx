Taking notes on your laptop and then needing them on your phone shouldn't mean building two separate apps. Classnotes Buddy solves that by keeping a Next.js web app and an Expo mobile app in one Turborepo workspace, built on top of the create-t3-turbo stack, so both share the same Supabase-backed notes workflow.

The Supabase setup is documented in detail: auth redirect rules, SQL triggers that keep user profiles in sync, and environment templates for local and preview deployments. Turbo runs dev, build, lint, and typecheck across both apps, so the monorepo stays fast even as it grows.
