Classnotes Buddy is a place to upload class notes and share them with a link. Your notes stay in sync when you open them on another device.

## Why it exists

I wanted a simple way to share notes without sending photos through a group chat or losing a folder link. Upload the notes once, then share the link.

## How it works

Supabase handles email and OAuth sign-in. Drizzle manages the Postgres database, and uploads sync across devices. The project is a Turborepo workspace with a Next.js web app and an Expo mobile app in progress.

## Stack

- Turborepo, pnpm workspaces, TypeScript.
- Supabase Auth + Postgres, Drizzle ORM.
- Next.js web client, with an Expo mobile app in progress.
