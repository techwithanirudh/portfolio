Classnotes Buddy lets you upload class notes and share them with a link, with everything synced instantly across your devices.

## Why it exists

Sharing class notes usually means a group chat full of photos of a whiteboard, or a folder link nobody can find later. This gives notes a proper home: upload once, get a link, and it's synced everywhere you open it.

## How it works

Uploads sync instantly across devices, with Supabase handling auth (email and OAuth) and profile syncing behind the scenes, and Drizzle managing the Postgres schema. It's built on a Turborepo workspace extending the create-t3-turbo stack, with a Next.js web client today and an Expo mobile app in progress.

## Stack

- Turborepo, pnpm workspaces, TypeScript.
- Supabase Auth + Postgres, Drizzle ORM.
- Next.js web client, with an Expo mobile app in progress.
