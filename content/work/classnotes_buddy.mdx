Classnotes Buddy lets you upload class notes and share them with a link, with everything synced instantly across your devices. It's built on a Turborepo workspace extending the create-t3-turbo stack, with Supabase handling auth and Drizzle managing the database schema. Mobile support (via Expo) is in progress.

Turbo pipelines handle dev/build/lint/typecheck so the monorepo stays fast as more apps get added to the workspace.
