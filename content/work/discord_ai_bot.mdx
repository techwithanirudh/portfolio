Discord AI Bot answers questions in text channels and can join voice channels to listen and talk.

## Why it exists

I wanted a Discord bot that could use different AI providers and handle voice as well as text. The bot is also easy to extend with new commands.

## How it works

Slash commands handle text messages. The Vercel AI SDK connects the bot to OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or Google. Voice uses speech-to-text and text-to-speech, and the bot also has web search and memory. Upstash Redis handles rate limits.

## Stack

- Bun, TypeScript, discord.js.
- OpenAI/Anthropic/OpenRouter/Google providers.
- Upstash Redis, Pino, ESLint/Prettier.
