Not everyone wants to build a portfolio site from scratch. Shadcn Portfolio is a starter template for that: a Next.js 15 site with shadcn/ui components and MDX-driven content, laid out in clean sections with Framer Motion handling the transitions, plus a blog and work pipeline ready to fill in.

It's a TurboRepo monorepo, so linting and formatting stay consistent across every package. SEO metadata, sitemaps, and structured data are already wired up, which means it can go straight into production for a personal site or an agency portfolio with little extra setup.
